Questions

Is the free version really free?

Yes. It's open source under the MIT licence, needs no signup, has no repo limit and runs on your machine for as long as you like.

What's the difference between self-hosted and Cloud?

Self-hosted runs on your own computer and GitHub, like an app you install: your code never leaves. Cloud is run for you, like Gmail: connect a repo and we check every change and watch your live site.

Do you ever see my code?

Not with self-hosted. With a licence key it sends only the key and a one-way repo fingerprint. Cloud will read your code in an isolated runner, delete it within minutes and keep only the findings.

What does one-time mean?

Pay once and use it for as long as you like. The price includes a year of new checks. After that you can renew, or keep the version you have, forever.

When do paid plans open?

Soon. Join the waitlist and we'll email you once, when your plan opens, with launch pricing.

What if it isn't right for me?

Ask within 14 days of your first payment for a full refund. Non-profits and students get a discount: email support@sigship.dev.
