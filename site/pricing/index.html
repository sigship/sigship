Questions

Is the free version really free?

Yes. It's open source (MIT) and runs on your computer. No account, no limits on projects, no card.

Do you upload my code?

Never in the free version: it makes no network calls at all. With a licence key it sends only the key and a one-way repo fingerprint. Cloud will read only the apps you connect, in an isolated runner, and you can disconnect any time.

Does a 'Ready' mean my app is 100% safe?

No tool can promise that. It means our 17 checks found nothing blocking launch. It does not prove the app is safe.

What does one-time mean?

Pay once and use it for as long as you like. The price includes a year of new checks. After that you can renew, or keep the version you have.

When do paid plans open?

Soon. Join the waitlist and we'll email you once, when your plan opens, with launch pricing.

What if it isn't right for me?

Ask within 14 days of your first payment for a full refund.
