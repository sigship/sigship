Add it to your site

The Self-checked badge comes with the free check, today. Type your site’s address, copy the code, and paste it into your footer. The badge carries your site’s own ID and links to its record on sigship.dev. The live badges arrive with Cloud and are drawn by sigship.dev.

Badge ID SG-S-P35GZZ
Paste into your site

Anyone can open a badge’s record at sigship.dev/verify and see what it means and who checked. A badge means the owner checked before launching, or that SIGSHIP or an expert did, as the level says. It isn’t a security certificate, and the record says so. What an expert review covers is written down in the review protocol, and the incident rule says what happens if a badge fails you.
