Check your app in 4 steps. 

Never used a terminal? No problem. This takes about two minutes.

1

Get your code

In Lovable or Bolt, connect GitHub and download your project. In Cursor or Replit, it's already on your computer.

2

Open a terminal in that folder

On Mac: right-click the folder → New Terminal at Folder. On Windows: type cmd in the folder's address bar.

3

Run the check

Paste the line below and press Enter. It takes a second.

npx sigship
4

Paste the fixes into your AI

Run it with --prompt, copy everything, paste it into your AI tool's chat. Check again until it says Ready.

npx sigship --prompt
