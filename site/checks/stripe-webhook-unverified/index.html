SEV-1: blocks launch

Your Stripe webhook accepts anything

What goes wrong

Someone posts a fake "payment succeeded" event to your endpoint and gets your paid plan for free.

How to fix it

Verify every event with stripe.webhooks.constructEvent, using the raw request body and your webhook signing secret, before you act on it.
