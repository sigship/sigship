A secret may be going to your logs

In plain English: Your app may be writing a password or secret key into its logs, the running notes your server keeps. More people and tools can usually read logs than your database.

What happens if you ship it

Passwords or tokens end up in a log service that more people can read than your database.

How to fix it Technical

Remove this log line, or log only that the action happened.

Questions

Why are secrets in logs a problem?

Logs are copied to hosting dashboards and log services, kept for a long time and shared with teammates, so a secret in a log spreads quietly.

What should I log instead?

That the action happened, and maybe who did it, but never the password, token or key itself.
